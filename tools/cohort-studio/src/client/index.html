    What am I looking at?
    
              
    
                These are made-up people. Synthea is a simulator: it invents residents of the chosen state and lives
                out their medical lives year by year (birth, check-ups, illnesses, prescriptions, hospital stays and
                sometimes death) using published statistics, so the overall picture resembles a population. No real person
                is in here, and nothing here describes real patients.
              
    
              
    
              
    
                
    EncounterAny contact with healthcare: a check-up, an emergency visit, a hospital stay, a call.
    
                
    ConditionA diagnosis written in the record, e.g. "Hypertension" or "Viral sinusitis".
    
                
    ObservationOne measurement: a blood pressure reading, a weight, a lab value, a survey answer.
    
                
    Medication requestA prescription. One medicine can be prescribed many times.
